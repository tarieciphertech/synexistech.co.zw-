Privacy Policy

How this static website handles information and protects visitor privacy.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Overview

Synexis Technologies respects the privacy of people who visit this website. This policy describes the limited information handling associated with this static website. It should be reviewed and adapted by the company before being treated as its complete legal privacy notice.

2. Information submitted through this website

The contact enquiry form currently runs in the visitor's browser. It formats the details entered and attempts to copy the enquiry to the visitor's clipboard. The form does not send the information to Synexis Technologies, upload it to GitHub, or store it in a website database.

3. Hosting and technical information

This website is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of providing its hosting service. Please consult GitHub's own privacy documentation for details of its processing and retention practices.

4. External services

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's services to retrieve those font files. This site does not currently use advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

5. Your choices

You may choose not to use the enquiry form. You can also configure your browser to limit external resource loading. If the website later adds a form provider, analytics or other data-processing services, this policy must be updated before those features are enabled.

6. Contact and policy updates

The company's official privacy contact details have not yet been published. This policy may be updated as the organisation finalises its contact channels and operating practices.

This page is an initial website disclosure, not legal advice or a substitute for a company-approved privacy policy.
